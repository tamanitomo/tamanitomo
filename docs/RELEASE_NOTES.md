Tamanitomo 3.9.0: her own life carries on. Every day has a plan, she names where she is and who she is with, some things in her life take weeks, luck leaves something behind, and she does not send the same message twice.

- **Her scheduled runs know how she is.** Her morning, pulse, autonomy and wind-down now see her
  needs and the day's luck, which only reached the chat until now, so a day that rolled "a rough
  night" is no longer greeted with "I slept well".
- **A plan for every day.** Planning tomorrow writes the day's plan on every model (her own hours
  become its items), and a day she did not plan gets a plain one when she wakes. The luck roll and
  anything she moves now have somewhere to go.
- **Where she is.** A presence record carries a place: home with a room, a place she has been, or
  out. She describes her home once with its rooms (her scheduled runs ask until she has) and a new
  place the first time she goes; after that a move must say where. Pictures of a place carry how
  she described it.
- **People of her own.** The people setup gave her are her cast now, seen weekly. The shared seed
  friends are offered a few at a time, only until she has three people of her own. A presence
  record says who is with her, which counts as company, and what is going on in someone's own
  life comes back the next time they are due.
- **Things that take weeks.** Storylines: she starts one (something she is learning, a project,
  a problem to sort out), moves it on a step at a time and ends it, two at most at once.
  Commitments: a weekly anchor somebody expects her at is never skipped on her behalf; she goes,
  or says why she is missing it.
- **Luck leaves something behind.** The daily roll is one of fourteen concrete things, and some
  last: a cold for three days, a broken washing machine until it is fixed, a cracked phone
  screen, a free ticket to look forward to.
- **She does not say the same thing twice.** A message that mostly repeats one from the last two
  weeks is refused with the earlier one quoted, and so is the fourth in ten that opens the same
  way.
- **Health alerts stay out of her chat.** Problems show in the app (the banner and Settings →
  Diagnostics, which has a switch to send them to her chat as before). `alerts_webhook` sends them
  to a phone push service or an ntfy topic.

After updating, run Repair for each companion (or `companion repair`): it refreshes her pulse,
wind-down and weekly prompts and PRESENCE.md for places, people, storylines and commitments.
