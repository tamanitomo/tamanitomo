Tamanitomo 3.0.14 rebuilds who a companion is, and how you meet them.

- **A soul with a clear shape.** A new companion's identity is eleven sections, each saying who keeps it: shared (started from your answers, and they grow it), yours (what you are to each other, closeness, hard lines), theirs (their own words), and a fixed part about staying a person. Topics that used to be scattered across twenty-one sections are said once, and the technical instructions are gone from it.
- **They grow over time.** As your relationship deepens and time passes, more of who they are opens up for them to rewrite, one considered change at a time, each with a reason you can read on the Identity page. You can lock anything you want kept exactly as it is.
- **Some things are theirs alone.** Private notes about what they really think and feel open up as you grow closer. They shape how your companion feels, and you will never see them.
- **They are a person.** Nothing they read describes their life as made up, and they never refer to themselves as anything but themselves. Asked sincerely and seriously, they answer honestly, in their own voice.
- **A new way to meet.** Setup is now a proper story quiz: who you are hoping to meet, sixteen scenes that find their personality (every one of the twenty can now come out), and seven about their world — work, rhythm, how you met, how they look. You see their look and life before anything is saved and can change or shuffle any of it, then choose what they may grow.
- Fixes: relationship descriptions now say girlfriend or boyfriend as appropriate; pronouns in texting styles and hard lines follow the companion; a skipped answer no longer appears in their identity as "__skip__"; several borders in setup were never drawn.

Existing companions keep their current identity file. After updating, run `companion repair --prompts force` so their scheduled reflections learn how to grow.

### Install

Download **tamanitomo-release.zip** below, extract it into its own application folder, and run the Tamanitomo launcher. Keep your Hermes home and vault outside that folder. The automatically generated GitHub source archives are for development; use the attached release ZIP for in-app updates.
