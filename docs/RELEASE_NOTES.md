Tamanitomo 2.8.0 gives a companion something to want, and lets her think with the model you chose.

**She now has ideas, and they are not the same seven every day.** A companion had a daily rhythm of about seven generic anchors, five scripted alternative days, and a one-in-a-hundred chance of a spontaneous detour. Nothing ever pushed against the safest answer, so the safest answer is what came back — she stayed in and read, more or less indefinitely. There is now a palette of more than eighty ideas: places to walk, things to make, people to feed, small adventures, and the good kind of doing nothing. A handful are offered to her each evening, weighted away from whatever she has done lately and toward the season and whether tomorrow has room for a long day out.

They are invitations. She takes one, combines two, ignores them entirely, or records something of her own — and anything she invents joins the palette and comes round again, slightly favoured, because it was hers. Anything she does not want is never offered again. What she picks is remembered by name, so "recently" is a fact rather than something guessed at from her own description of it.

**Background work uses the model you configured.** Until now every background job carried a model endpoint written into the job itself, with no relationship to the companion's own setting — one could be thinking with a small model on another machine entirely while her settings said otherwise. These jobs speak plain HTTP, so the only providers they could ever reach were ones with a URL and a key, which ruled out exactly the ones most people use. They now resolve the model from the companion's own configuration, and reach providers signed in through Hermes the same way image generation already does.

### Install

Download **tamanitomo-release.zip** below, extract it into its own application folder, and run the Tamanitomo launcher. Keep your Hermes home and vault outside that folder. The automatically generated GitHub source archives are for development; use the attached release ZIP for in-app updates.
